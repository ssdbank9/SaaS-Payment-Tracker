Settings

Shown in the header, the browser tab and the home-screen icon (a monogram of the first letter). Leave blank for Wasool. Shown next to the name in the header and in the browser tab title. Leave blank for Know who's paid. The Claude model that reads statement screenshots, pasted statement text and Quick add lines. Quick is the cheapest and fastest and is enough for clean screenshots; switch to Default if it misreads amounts or dates. Payments and costs saved without their own rate use this default, so changing it here re-values all of them in the summary tiles and chart. An entry with its own rate keeps it. Rows in the tables always show each entry's own currency; the tiles amalgamate into PKR and USD. From this day until the reply-by day the page flags a reminder run: everyone with an amount due for the next cycle gets a WhatsApp nudge, and you tick who was reminded and who confirmed. The day people should confirm or pay by, before the cycle starts on the anchor day. Used for {reply_by} in the message. How a mid-cycle first period is charged. Actual days: price × days ÷ days in that cycle (31 for Aug 24 – Sep 23). Fixed 30-day month: a daily rate of price ÷ 30, capped at the full price (Rs 4,200 → Rs 140 a day; 22 days = Rs 3,080). The days charged can also be edited on each plan's first period. Every plan bills on the same cycle: monthly periods run from this day of the month to the day before the next one, and yearly periods from this date each year. Someone who joins mid-period pays a prorated share of that first period (by days), unless proration is turned off on their plan.

Packages & products

Every plan belongs to one package or product. The billing cycle sets how long its periods are (monthly, yearly, or a one-time amount) on the shared cycle anchor above; a plan can override it. A default price is optional and only prefills new plans.

Placeholders: {name_or_email} {package} {plan} {amount_due} (next cycle, after credit) {period} (e.g. Sep 24 – Oct 23) {due_date} {reply_by} {balance_due} {periods_missing}. For someone who is overdue, {amount_due}, {period} and {due_date} describe the unpaid period instead. Reminders open WhatsApp with this text prefilled; nothing is sent automatically.

Same placeholders. In the final notice {due_date} is the day the new cycle starts (the deactivation day), and {amount_due} and {period} describe the unpaid amount for someone who is overdue.

Users and payments

One row per payment (or per plan with no payment yet). Columns: name and/or email, join_date; optional phone, telegram, notes, package, plan (label), prorate (yes/no), discount (e.g. 10% or 500 per period), status (active or cancelled) with last_day, kind (monthly or one-time), currency (USD or PKR), price, kind may also be yearly, plan_start, plan_end (or due date), due_by, payment_date, payment_amount, payment_currency, payment_rate, payment_note. Existing users (by email, then name) and plans (by name) are updated; a payment on an existing date replaces it.
