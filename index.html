Packages & products

Every plan belongs to one package or product. The billing cycle sets how long its periods are (monthly, yearly, or a one-time amount) on the shared cycle anchor above; a plan can override it. A default price is optional and only prefills new plans.

Placeholders: {name_or_email} {package} {plan} {amount_due} (next cycle, after credit) {period} (e.g. Sep 24 – Oct 23) {due_date} {reply_by} {balance_due} {periods_missing} {confirm_link} (the user's one-tap YES/NO page; needs the Public base URL below, and is added to the end of the message automatically when the template does not mention it). For someone who is overdue, {amount_due}, {period} and {due_date} describe the unpaid period instead. Reminders open WhatsApp with this text prefilled; nothing is sent automatically.

Same placeholders. In the final notice {due_date} is the day the new cycle starts (the deactivation day), and {amount_due} and {period} describe the unpaid amount for someone who is overdue.

Queue messages

The Queue picks today's stage from the date: stage 1 from the reminder day, stage 2 from the reply-by day to anyone without an answer, stage 3 on the cycle start to anyone still silent (their account continues at the current tier). Placeholders: {name} {amount} {cycle} (e.g. 24 Sep – 23 Oct 2026) {link} (the user's private choice page) {tier} {dueDate} {replyBy} {switchLabel} (Upgrade to C Max / Move back to C) {payHow}. The link is added to the end when a message does not mention it. The same texts are used by the daily email job.

Mail (daily reminder emails)

Kept on the server only, never in exports. With an SMTP account set here, the server job runs every day at 09:00 (Asia/Karachi) and emails today's stage to every user with an email address who has not had it yet for this cycle, then ticks them as sent in the Queue. For Gmail use smtp.gmail.com, port 587 and an app password. Leave it empty to send everything by hand.

Security

Your private sign-in address

The sign-in form exists only at this address. Anyone who opens your domain without it sees a blank page, so bookmark it or add it to your home screen. Your signed-in phones and computers are remembered and find it from the plain address by themselves.

…Open

This device

You stay signed in for 90 days, longer while you keep using the tracker.

Every device

Ends every signed-in session at once (a lost phone, a shared computer). You sign in again here afterwards.

Wrong passcodes lock only the device that typed them (8 tries in 15 minutes), and a flood of attempts from many devices is slowed down together; your own signed-in devices are never affected.

Your data

Everything the tracker stores (users, payments, costs, reminder ticks and settings) as one JSON file. Export from the claude.ai copy, then import on your own server to move in two clicks. Importing replaces the users and costs with the file's contents.
